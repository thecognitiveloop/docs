# Frequently Asked Questions


## What is The Cognitive Loop?

The Cognitive Loop is a cognitive infrastructure that captures, structures, validates, and activates your organization's operational knowledge. It transforms everyday decisions and expert judgment into reusable organizational assets.



## What is Cleo?

Cleo is the interactive cognitive companion built on The Cognitive Loop platform.

She helps professionals learn through real business scenarios, identifies cognitive gaps, supports onboarding, upskilling, and reskilling, and provides contextual guidance based on your organization's expertise.



## How is this different from a traditional knowledge base?

Traditional documentation stores information.

The Cognitive Loop captures decision-making criteria, operational judgment, and expert reasoning, making organizational knowledge reusable by both people and AI agents.



## Do you replace employees with AI?

No.

Our philosophy is Human in the Loop. AI assists people by accelerating learning, preserving organizational knowledge, and supporting decision-making. Humans remain responsible for critical decisions.



## What types of organizations benefit most?

Organizations with operational complexity, specialized expertise, frequent onboarding, distributed teams, regulated industries, or significant knowledge dependency on experienced professionals.



## Can the platform integrate with our existing systems?

Yes.

The platform is designed to integrate with existing enterprise systems when needed, while also supporting standalone deployments depending on organizational requirements.



## Who owns the knowledge captured in the platform?

Your organization.

Customer knowledge, documents, decision criteria, and operational intelligence remain the property of the customer.



## Is our information used to train public AI models?

No.

Customer proprietary information is never used to train public artificial intelligence models.



## How do you ensure the quality of captured knowledge?

Knowledge is validated through Human in the Loop processes, subject matter experts, peer review, and organizational governance before becoming part of your cognitive infrastructure.


## Is The Cognitive Loop secure?

Security is a foundational principle of our platform.

Our architecture follows Security by Design and Privacy by Design principles, with enterprise-grade controls for data protection and governance.



## Does the platform work only with AI agents?

No.

The Cognitive Loop is designed for both people and AI agents. Every captured cognitive asset can be reused by professionals, teams, and intelligent systems.



## How do we get started?

You can schedule a discovery session to evaluate your organization's knowledge flows, identify cognitive bottlenecks, and define an implementation strategy tailored to your operational reality.
