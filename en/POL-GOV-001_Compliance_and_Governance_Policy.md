## Compliance by Design

Our platform is designed with compliance as a foundational architectural principle rather than an operational afterthought.

Security, privacy, AI governance, and data management requirements are incorporated throughout the software lifecycle using recognized industry standards and regulatory frameworks.

Rather than implementing controls reactively, the platform follows a **design-first** approach in which governance requirements influence architecture, development, deployment, and operation.

This page provides an overview of the compliance framework supporting the platform.



## Compliance Framework

Our governance model is aligned with internationally recognized standards and regulations, including:

- SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria
- ISO/IEC 27001
- ISO/IEC 27701
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
- Privacy by Design principles
- Security by Design principles

Where applicable, controls are designed to support additional customer-specific regulatory requirements.



## Governance Model

The platform governance model is organized into four documentation layers.

| Layer | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| Policies | Define organizational commitments and governance principles. |
| Standards | Define mandatory technical and architectural requirements. |
| Procedures | Describe operational processes and recurring activities. |
| Guidelines | Provide implementation recommendations and best practices. |

This structure promotes consistency, auditability, and continuous improvement.



## Core Policies

The following policies establish the primary governance framework.

| Policy | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Information Security Policy | Organizational commitment to protecting information assets. |
| Data Governance & Processing Policy | Principles governing data collection, processing, ownership, retention, and protection. |
| AI Governance Policy | Principles governing responsible development and operation of AI capabilities. |



## Technical Standards

Our engineering practices are supported by mandatory design standards.

Current standards include:

- Data Classification
- Security by Design
- Privacy by Design
- Human-in-the-Loop
- Secure Software Development
- Identity and Access Management
- Encryption and Key Management
- Logging and Auditability
- Data Retention
- Data Deletion

Each standard defines technical requirements applicable during system design and operation.



## Security Principles

Security controls are incorporated throughout the platform lifecycle.

Examples include:

- Least privilege
- Defense in depth
- Secure defaults
- Encryption in transit
- Encryption at rest
- Identity-based access control
- Continuous monitoring
- Audit logging
- Risk-based access management



## Privacy Principles

Personal data is handled according to privacy engineering principles.

Key objectives include:

- Data minimization
- Purpose limitation
- Storage limitation
- Transparency
- User control
- Privacy by Design
- Privacy by Default
- Data segregation
- Anonymization and pseudonymization where appropriate



## AI Governance

Artificial intelligence capabilities are developed under controlled governance principles.

These include:

- Human oversight
- Explainability where applicable
- Controlled model usage
- Customer data isolation
- Training data governance
- Prompt security
- Retrieval isolation
- Continuous monitoring

Customer information is never used for foundation model training unless explicitly authorized by contract.



## Data Protection

The platform incorporates technical and organizational measures designed to protect customer information throughout its lifecycle.

These measures include:

- Data classification
- Tenant isolation
- Encryption
- Access control
- Auditability
- Secure deletion
- Retention management
- Backup protection



## Continuous Compliance

Compliance is treated as an ongoing process rather than a point-in-time certification effort.

Controls are periodically reviewed through:

- Internal security reviews
- Architecture reviews
- Risk assessments
- Secure development practices
- Vendor assessments
- Continuous improvement activities



## Transparency

We believe customers should understand how their information is managed.

Our governance documentation is intended to provide transparency regarding:

- platform architecture
- security principles
- privacy controls
- AI governance
- operational practices

Additional technical documentation may be made available upon request or under applicable customer agreements.
