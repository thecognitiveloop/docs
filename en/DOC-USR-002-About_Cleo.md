# Cleo

### Your Cognitive Learning Companion

>Learning is no longer separate from work.
>Cleo is the learning experience built on top of The Cognitive Loop's cognitive infrastructure, transforming everyday operational activity into continuous professional development. Rather than delivering static courses, Cleo develops operational judgment by exposing people to the same uncertainty, context, and decision criteria they will encounter in their daily work.
>Our objective is not simply to transfer knowledge, but to accelerate the development of professional reasoning.



## Learning Through Work

Traditional corporate training often separates learning from execution.

Cleo reverses that model.

Every onboarding process, operational procedure, expert interaction, and business case becomes an opportunity to learn, validate judgment, and strengthen organizational knowledge.

Learning occurs while solving real organizational problems, allowing knowledge acquisition to remain contextualized to the organization's own operational reality.



## Case-Based Learning

Operational expertise is developed through experience.

Cleo reproduces this process using Case-Based Learning methodologies, where collaborators confront realistic business situations instead of memorizing procedures.

Each scenario introduces uncertainty, competing priorities, incomplete information, and contextual constraints similar to those faced by experienced professionals.

The objective is not finding the correct answer, but understanding why an expert would choose one decision over another.


## Adaptive Learning

Every professional starts from a different level of experience.

Cleo continuously identifies cognitive gaps and adapts learning paths according to each individual's existing capabilities, previous knowledge, and demonstrated operational judgment.

Rather than forcing everyone through identical training, Cleo personalizes progression based on what each person actually needs to develop.

Learning becomes dynamic instead of sequential.



## Andragogy

Adults do not learn like students.

Cleo is designed around principles of adult learning (Andragogy), recognizing that professionals learn more effectively when education is immediately relevant to their work, builds upon prior experience, and solves concrete operational problems.

Instead of abstract theory, learning begins with real organizational situations.

Knowledge is acquired because it becomes useful, not because it appears in a curriculum.



## Heutagogy

Modern organizations require autonomous learners.

Beyond andragogy, Cleo incorporates principles of Heutagogy—self-determined learning—encouraging professionals to actively explore, question, and refine their own decision-making criteria.

The objective is not merely competence, but capability: the ability to transfer judgment to unfamiliar situations and continuously adapt as organizations evolve.



## Competency Mapping

Effective learning begins with understanding what people already know.

Cleo evaluates existing competencies and maps professional capabilities using internationally recognized competency frameworks, including adaptations of SFIA 9 and other organizational capability models.

This allows the platform to:

- Identify existing strengths.
- Detect competency gaps.
- Measure progression over time.
- Recommend personalized learning paths.
- Support career development.
- Accelerate role transitions.

Organizations may also incorporate their own competency models alongside industry standards.



## Personalized Learning Paths

No two professionals require the same learning journey.

Using competency mapping, organizational context, and operational objectives, Cleo generates individualized learning trajectories that evolve continuously.

Recommendations are based on:

- Existing experience.
- Required capabilities.
- Organizational priorities.
- Operational performance.
- Expert validation.
- Learning history.

Every interaction contributes to refining future recommendations.



## Accelerating Onboarding

Most organizational knowledge already exists.

It is simply fragmented across documents, procedures, meetings, experts, and informal practices.

Cleo transforms existing organizational content—including documentation, operational procedures, business processes, policies, knowledge bases, and expert criteria—into structured onboarding experiences.

Business processes become guided learning journeys instead of static documentation.

New collaborators develop operational autonomy significantly faster because they learn the reasoning behind each decision, not only the sequence of tasks.



## Upskilling and Reskilling

As organizations evolve, people must evolve with them.

Cleo supports continuous upskilling and reskilling by connecting existing knowledge with new operational requirements.

Rather than restarting from zero, new competencies are built upon previously acquired capabilities, reducing learning time and improving knowledge retention.



## Human Expertise at Scale

Every interaction with Cleo contributes to strengthening the organization's cognitive infrastructure.

Experts no longer teach one person at a time.

Their reasoning, decision criteria, exceptions, and accumulated judgment become reusable organizational assets that can be shared across teams, locations, and future AI agents while preserving human oversight.


## Learning That Creates Organizational Assets

Traditional learning ends when the course finishes.

Learning with Cleo permanently enriches the organization's cognitive infrastructure.

Every validated interaction simultaneously develops people, preserves expertise, and strengthens the collective intelligence of the organization.

Learning is no longer a cost center.

It becomes the mechanism through which organizations continuously build their most valuable strategic asset: accumulated operational judgment.
