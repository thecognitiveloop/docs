# Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how The Cognitive Loop ("we", "our", or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

By continuing to use our website, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy, except where your browser settings or applicable law require otherwise.



## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser.

They allow websites to recognize your device, remember preferences, improve performance, and collect anonymous usage statistics.



## How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

- Keep the website functioning properly.
- Remember basic user preferences.
- Measure website performance.
- Understand how visitors interact with our content.
- Improve the user experience.
- Maintain security features.



## Types of Cookies

### Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function correctly.

Without them, some services may not be available.

Examples include:

- Session management
- Authentication
- Security
- Load balancing



### Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use our website.

Information collected is aggregated whenever possible and used only to improve our services.

Examples may include:

- Page visits
- Session duration
- Navigation paths
- Device type



### Functional Cookies

These cookies remember choices you make to provide a more personalized experience.

Examples include:

- Language preference
- User interface preferences



## Third-Party Cookies

Some services integrated into our website may place their own cookies.

Examples may include analytics providers, embedded media, or customer support services.

Their use is governed by the privacy policies of those providers.



## Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to:

- View stored cookies.
- Delete existing cookies.
- Block future cookies.
- Configure cookie preferences.

Blocking certain cookies may affect website functionality.
